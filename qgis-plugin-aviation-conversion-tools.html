AviationConversionTools

QGIS plugin to convert between units used in aviation (NM, km, feet, etc.) and various coordinate formats (DMS, DD, ARINC424 full degrees)

Python  ·  PyQGIS  ·  QGIS

The problem

Aviation data uses a variety of measurement units and coordinate formats. Depending on the source, information may be expressed in different units of distance, altitude or speed, while geographic positions may use different coordinate notations.

Switching between these formats manually can interrupt workflows, require repetitive calculations and introduce conversion or transcription errors.

The solution

Aviation Conversion Tool provides a dedicated conversion workflow within QGIS, helping users transform aviation-related measurements and geographic coordinates between supported formats.

By bringing these conversions into the GIS environment, the plugin makes it easier to prepare, interpret and work with aviation data without repeatedly relying on external tools.
